* What is the difference between VoltIQ Home, Installer and Fleet pricing?

Each tier works differently, so here is exactly what moves each one.

  • Home covers one home at R99. A second home is charged again, and regular scheduled reporting is an add-on. Alerts themselves are included.
  • Installer covers every system you have fitted for one price. Adding installations does not move it. Scheduled reporting on top of the standard WhatsApp message is the add-on.
  • Fleet is priced per large site, so five sites is five times R999. Additional reports, early fault warnings, upsell flagging and white-label branding are each priced on top.

Your team size never moves any of them. Multi-user access on Fleet is included whatever the headcount.

Straight Talk

What VoltIQ does not do

Monitoring software that oversells itself gets installers into trouble. Here is where the line sits, in the same words as our terms.

It doesn't replace a site visit

Alerts, upsell flags, and morning reports are advisory. They do not replace site visits, electrical inspections, certified compliance checks, or licensed installer judgment. The install, repair, and commercial decisions stay yours.

Detection is best-effort

False positives and missed events can occur, particularly during firmware updates, extreme weather, or upstream API issues. We keep improving the detection logic, and we do not warrant that it is complete or accurate.

We depend on upstream portals

VoltIQ reads from each manufacturer's portal. Outages, rate limits, or breaking API changes on their side are excluded from our uptime target. We restore service as fast as the upstream allows.

The full picture, including WhatsApp opt-in obligations and intellectual property, is in the VoltIQ terms.
